International trade & investment

Legal support for commercial and investment activities across borders, taking account of differences between national laws, regulations and cross-border requirements.

Legal services

  • International commercial transactions, delivery terms and contractual liability.
  • Foreign investment and related legal and regulatory requirements.
  • Private international law, conflict of laws and jurisdiction.
  • International contracts, governing law and dispute resolution mechanisms.
  • Citizenship or residence matters linked to investment, where available.
  • Business formation and partnerships in foreign markets.
  • Legal services for companies operating in multiple countries.
  • Review of legal risks in cross-border investments and partnerships.

How can we help?

  • Identify jurisdictions and laws that may be relevant to a transaction.
  • Review international agreements and the parties’ mutual obligations.
  • Explain initial legal requirements for entering a new market.
  • Assess risks associated with foreign investment and international partnerships.
  • Coordinate with local counsel when a procedure or legal opinion in another country is required.

Who can benefit?

Foreign investors, international companies, importers and exporters, entrepreneurs, and individuals with interests connected to more than one country.
