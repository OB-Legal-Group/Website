International trade & investment

Legal support for commercial and investment activities across borders, taking account of differences between national laws, regulations and cross-border requirements.

Legal services

  • International commercial transactions, delivery terms and contractual liability.
  • Foreign investment and the related legal and regulatory requirements.
  • Private international law, conflict of laws and jurisdiction.
  • International contracts, choice of governing law and dispute resolution mechanisms.
  • Setting up businesses and partnerships in foreign markets.
  • Legal services for companies operating in more than one country.
  • Reviewing legal risks in cross-border investments and partnerships.

How can we help?

  • Identifying the countries and laws that may be relevant to the transaction.
  • Reviewing international agreements and the mutual obligations of the parties.
  • Explaining the initial legal requirements for entering a new market.
  • Assessing risks linked to foreign investment and international partnerships.
  • Coordinating with partner local lawyers when procedures or a legal opinion are needed in another country.

Who can benefit?

Foreign investors, international companies, exporters and importers, entrepreneurs, and individuals with interests in more than one country.

Where we work and what we cover

  • We currently work in: Turkey, the UAE and Syria.
  • Comprehensive coverage: Saudi Arabia, Qatar and Egypt.
  • Partial coverage: other Arab countries and the European Union.
