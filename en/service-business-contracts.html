Companies & business

Legal support for businesses throughout their lifecycle, from formation and partner arrangements to contracts, commercial transactions, governance, expansion and restructuring.

Legal services

  • Company formation and choosing the legal structure according to the country concerned.
  • Corporate governance, defining powers, responsibilities and decision-making mechanisms.
  • Legal and regulatory compliance for companies and business activities, depending on the country concerned.
  • Commercial law and relationships with customers, suppliers and partners.
  • Drafting, reviewing and auditing service, supply, distribution, agency and partnership contracts.
  • Reviewing the legal aspects of mergers and acquisitions.
  • Corporate restructuring and organizing legal and contractual structures.
  • Partner and shareholder agreements, and the related rights and obligations.

How can we help?

  • Explaining formation requirements and the options available in each country.
  • Reviewing contracts before signing and identifying obligations and risks.
  • Organizing the relationship between partners, management and shareholders.
  • Assessing legal issues before entering a partnership or transaction.
  • Identifying compliance requirements, or the need for a specialist local lawyer.

Who can benefit?

Startups, small and medium-sized businesses, entrepreneurs, international companies, shareholders and investors.

Scope of service

Formation and registration steps depend on the country concerned and are carried out with partner local lawyers where needed.

Where we work and what we cover

  • We currently work in: Turkey, the UAE and Syria.
  • Comprehensive coverage: Saudi Arabia, Qatar and Egypt.
  • Partial coverage: other Arab countries and the European Union.
