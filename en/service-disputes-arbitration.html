Litigation & dispute resolution

Legal support to assess disputes and manage available resolution options, from negotiation and settlement to litigation or arbitration, depending on the matter and available professional scope.

Legal services

  • Civil and commercial disputes involving rights and obligations.
  • Legal claims arising from breach of contract or failure to perform obligations.
  • Commercial arbitration, including review of arbitration clauses and related documents.
  • Mediation and negotiation to explore consensual solutions.
  • Dispute settlement and preparation or review of settlement agreements.
  • Contract disputes concerning interpretation, performance or termination.
  • Cross-border issues involving jurisdiction and foreign judgments.

How can we help?

  • Review facts and documents and identify the main points in dispute.
  • Assess available routes and the potential risks of each.
  • Assist with claims and related legal correspondence.
  • Support negotiations and evaluate settlement proposals.
  • Clarify the scope of available support in litigation or arbitration.

Who can benefit?

Businesses, individuals, investors, commercial partners and parties to domestic or cross-border disputes.
