Real estate

Legal services for real estate transactions and investments, including checking property registries.

Legal services

  • Real estate investment and assessing the legal issues linked to investment opportunities.
  • Reviewing sale, purchase, lease and real estate development contracts.
  • Legal due diligence on documents, rights and restrictions affecting a property, including checking property registries.
  • Ownership, lease, management, development and real estate investment agreements.
  • Real estate transactions involving foreign parties under the relevant law.
  • Citizenship through real estate investment, in Turkey only.
  • Assessing legal options in real estate disputes.

How can we help?

  • Reviewing contracts and documents before completing real estate transactions.
  • Checking property registries and identifying legal risks and obligations.
  • Explaining the legal requirements relevant to foreign investors.
  • Supporting citizenship procedures linked to real estate in Turkey.
  • Assessing the options when a real estate dispute arises.

Who can benefit?

Real estate investors, real estate companies, developers, and individuals who want to buy or invest in property.

Scope of service

We cover real estate in Turkey, Syria and the UAE, and partially in Saudi Arabia and Qatar. We check property registries ourselves. We do not provide residency services.
