Finance & debt matters

Legal support for financial claims, debts and payment disputes, as well as matters involving insolvency, restructuring obligations and tax issues.

Legal services

  • Debt collection and review of the documents supporting claims.
  • Financial disputes relating to loans, payments and dues.
  • Cross-border financial claims.
  • Bankruptcy, insolvency and the legal options when payment defaults.
  • Debt restructuring and negotiating repayment schedules.
  • Negotiating with creditors on settlement and amended obligations.
  • Tax matters and disputes, in coordination with specialists.
  • Reviewing guarantees, sureties and clauses linked to financial obligations.

How can we help?

  • Reviewing the documents and contracts that evidence a debt or financial obligation.
  • Assessing the legal options available for claiming dues.
  • Helping negotiate a settlement or reschedule debts.
  • Explaining the risks and likely procedures in cases of default or insolvency.
  • Identifying tax issues that need legal support or coordination with specialists.

Who can benefit?

Creditor and debtor companies, commercial institutions, investors, business owners, and individuals with financial claims or obligations.

Where we work and what we cover

  • We currently work in: Turkey, the UAE and Syria.
  • Comprehensive coverage: Saudi Arabia, Qatar and Egypt.
  • Partial coverage: other Arab countries and the European Union.
