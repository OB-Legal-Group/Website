Real estate & intellectual property

Legal services for real estate transactions and investments, protection of intellectual property rights, and agreements involving technology, innovation and intangible assets.

Legal services

  • Real estate investment and legal assessment of investment opportunities.
  • Review of sale, purchase, lease and property development agreements.
  • Legal due diligence on property documents, rights and restrictions.
  • Agreements for property ownership, leasing, management, development and investment.
  • Real estate transactions involving foreign parties, subject to applicable law.
  • Intellectual property rights, including trademarks, copyright and designs.
  • IP licensing, transfer and commercial-use agreements.
  • Technology contracts, software agreements and digital services arrangements.
  • Assessment of legal options in real estate or intellectual property disputes.

How can we help?

  • Review contracts and documents before completing real estate transactions.
  • Identify legal risks and obligations associated with property investment.
  • Explain relevant requirements for foreign investors.
  • Help structure ownership of intellectual property and licensing arrangements.
  • Review technology agreements and assess options if a dispute arises.

Who can benefit?

Real estate investors, property businesses, developers, trademark owners, technology companies, entrepreneurs and innovators.
